Privacy

Short, because there is little to say.

  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and collects nothing. There is no form to fill in.
  • The programs run on your own computer. Your shows, media and settings stay on that computer. LUMOS does not send them anywhere.
  • Callers and guests on a show's call-in line or guest link are connected through the show operator's own telephony and video accounts. LUMOS never records a call. Call records are deleted from the telephony account when the line is hung up. A guest's camera and microphone are sent only to the show, never stored.
  • Text messages are sent only to a caller who asks for one on the phone, one message, with STOP honoured. See the call-in line page.
  • Questions go to Matthew Warren via mattjwarren.com.
