The call-in line and its one text message

A show made with LUMOS Broadcaster can offer a toll-free call-in number. Here is exactly what happens on that line and the one text message it can send.

Who calls

A viewer who wants to appear as a guest dials the show's toll-free number of their own accord. Nobody is ever called or messaged first.

How consent to a text is given

On the call, the host asks: "If you would like to join with your camera, we can text this number a one-time link to open on your phone. Would you like us to send it?" Only if the caller says yes is a single text message sent from the show's number.

What the text says

LUMOS: tap to join the show with your camera: https://vdo.ninja/?room=... Reply STOP to opt out.

What never happens

  • No marketing, no reminders, no second message.
  • STOP is honored. HELP is answered.
  • Calls are not recorded. The call records are deleted from the telephony account when the line is hung up, so no caller's number is kept.
  • One text per caller, per call, and only during the call, at the caller's spoken request.
